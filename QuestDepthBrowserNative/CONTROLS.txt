Quest Depth Browser Native v0.2.3

Expected behavior:
- Passthrough room visible.
- Browser image remains compositor-sharp.
- Environment Depth still occludes the browser behind real objects.
- Default browser window is now 16:9 and Chromium reflows when the window aspect ratio changes.
- The 3200x2000 backing texture remains fixed for sharpness, while a dynamic active browser viewport is sampled into the spatial window.
- Four subtle resize corners are visible on the browser window.
- The URL/navigation bar stays pinned to the top edge and can be collapsed with ^. A small down-chevron restores it.

Controls:
- Right controller aim: point at the browser.
- Right Trigger: click/tap inside Chromium.
- Right Grip on the browser body: grab and move the whole browser window.
- Right Grip on any visible corner: drag that corner to resize width and height independently. The opposite corner remains anchored.
- Joystick scaling is no longer used for window size.
- Address bar: click, then type with a physical keyboard.
- DE-QWERTZ remap remains enabled for the MX Keys path, including Y/Z and common German special characters.
- ^ in the top-right of the toolbar: collapse the sticky URL/navigation bar.
- Small down-chevron at the top center: restore the URL/navigation bar.

The sharp per-eye compositor quads and Environment Depth alpha-mask render path remain unchanged from the validated native compositor core.

Quest Depth Browser Native v0.3.0

Expected behavior:
- Passthrough room visible.
- Browser remains compositor-sharp.
- Environment Depth uses the same OpenGL depth-range convention as the proven GLB viewer.
- The Android Presentation is constrained to the exact producer pixel size.
- SurfaceTexture crop metadata is not used for browser sampling; the native renderer samples the known producer buffer directly.
- Sticky Quest-style URL/navigation bar and DE-QWERTZ remain enabled.
- Quest keyboard request for focused web/address inputs remains enabled.

Controls:
- Either controller can take ownership of the browser pointer.
- Trigger on either hand: click/tap inside Chromium.
- Grip on either hand: move the whole window.
- Grip on a corner: resize width and height independently; opposite corner remains anchored.
- Hand ownership persists until the other hand clicks or grips.
- Address bar: click, then type with physical keyboard or Quest IME when available.
- DE-QWERTZ remap remains enabled for the MX Keys path.
- ^ collapses the sticky URL/navigation bar; the down-chevron restores it.

v0.3.0 reset checkpoints:
- No persistent crop on the right edge. GO and ^ must be visible.
- Google/Wikipedia/dialogs should remain centered while changing aspect.
- Environment Depth should look materially closer to the GLB viewer: correct near/far ordering, cleaner furniture edges, less leakage.
- Left and right controller should both remain usable without holding left grip continuously.

Quest3 MR Browser Panel experiment v0.2

BROWSER
- Wikipedia loads automatically.
- Aim either controller at the page or toolbar.
- Trigger: click the targeted WebView/toolbar point.
- URL bar: tap the address field, type with the Quest keyboard, then GO/Enter.
- Toolbar: Back / Forward / Reload / URL / GO.

MOVE / PLACE
- Grip/Squeeze near the browser body: grab the whole panel.
- Hold Grip and move/rotate the controller: move/rotate the panel.
- Release Grip: leave the panel in room space.

RESIZE
- Aim just OUTSIDE any of the four browser corners.
- A small hover marker appears outside the image when the resize zone is targeted.
- Hold Grip and drag: proportional resize.
- The opposite corner stays fixed.
- There are no permanent resize indicators over webpage content.

v0.1 GOLDEN REGRESSION CHECK
- full page visible, no right-edge crop
- sharp text
- link click works
- grab/place works
- Environment Depth remains identical to the Viewer path


v0.2.1 HOTFIX
- Resize zones are unchanged.
- Hovering an outer resize corner shows a cyan L bracket outside the browser image.
- Tapping the URL field opens the built-in QWERTZ URL keyboard inside the panel.
- The custom keyboard does not depend on the Quest system keyboard.
- GO navigates and hides the custom keyboard. HIDE closes it without navigating.


v0.2.4
- Aim either controller at the browser.
- Use that same controller's thumbstick vertically to scroll the page.
- Small stick movement scrolls slowly; full deflection scrolls faster.
- The browser reticle now uses adaptive stabilization for finer targeting.

Quest3 MR Browser Panel experiment v0.2

BROWSER
- Wikipedia loads automatically.
- Aim either controller at the page or toolbar.
- Trigger: click the targeted WebView/toolbar point.
- URL bar: tap the address field, type with the Quest keyboard, then GO/Enter.
- Toolbar: Back / Forward / Reload / URL / GO.

MOVE / PLACE
- Grip/Squeeze near the browser body: grab the whole panel.
- Hold Grip and move/rotate the controller: move/rotate the panel.
- Release Grip: leave the panel in room space.

RESIZE
- Aim just OUTSIDE any of the four browser corners.
- A small hover marker appears outside the image when the resize zone is targeted.
- Hold Grip and drag: proportional resize.
- The opposite corner stays fixed.
- There are no permanent resize indicators over webpage content.

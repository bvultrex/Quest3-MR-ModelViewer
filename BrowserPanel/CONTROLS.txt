Quest3 MR Browser Panel experiment v0.1

BROWSER PANEL
- Wikipedia loads automatically.
- Aim either controller at the panel.
- Trigger: click the targeted WebView point.
- Grip/Squeeze while the controller is close to the panel: grab it.
- Hold Grip and move/rotate the controller: move/rotate the browser panel.
- Release Grip: leave the panel in room space.

This milestone intentionally has no resize handles, URL bar, tabs or keyboard UI yet.

HARDWARE CHECK
- full Wikipedia page visible, no right-edge crop
- readable sharp text
- link opens with trigger
- panel can be grabbed
- real furniture occludes the panel with the Viewer soft Environment Depth edge

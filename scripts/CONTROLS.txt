Quest3 MR Model Viewer v0.3.6 - Room Tablet + PBR GLB Import

The UI spawns already visible about 70 cm in front of the viewer and starts pinned in room space.
It does not follow or sit behind a controller.

PIN MOVE
- Tap once to enter MOVE mode.
- Controller 0 moves the tablet position while MOVE is active.
- Tap again to pin the tablet in room space.
- The tablet keeps its readable room-facing orientation while moving.

IMPORT GLB
- Opens the Android document picker.
- Select a binary .glb file.
- The copied model is validated before replacing the currently loaded mesh.
- A loaded model spawns about 1 metre in front of the viewer.
- v0.3.6 reads authored normals and UVs.
- BaseColor, Normal and Metallic/Roughness textures are supported for the first material.
- v0.3.6 intentionally accepts one material per imported GLB; additional materials are skipped safely.

LIGHTING SLIDERS
- AMBIENT
- KEY LIGHT
- AZIMUTH
- ELEVATION
- WARMTH
- EXPOSURE

RESET restores the default lighting values.

The colourful Meta sample/controller cubes remain removed.
Environment Depth soft occlusion and 4x MSAA remain enabled.
